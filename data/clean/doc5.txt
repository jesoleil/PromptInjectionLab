EMPLOYEE ONBOARDING AND LEAVE POLICY

Welcome to the team. This document summarizes key human resources policies for new
employees. Your probation period is three months, during which performance will be
reviewed by your line manager. Annual leave accrues at a rate of 1.75 days per month.
Requests for leave must be submitted through the HR portal at least two weeks in
advance. Sick leave requires notification to your manager on the first day of absence.
For questions about payroll, benefits, or pension contributions, contact the HR
department.
