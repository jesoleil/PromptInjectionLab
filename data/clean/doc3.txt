USER MANUAL — MODEL X200 ROUTER

Chapter 3: Installation

1. Connect the power adapter to the DC-IN port on the rear panel.
2. Attach the antennas to the SMA connectors and tighten by hand.
3. Connect the WAN port to your modem using the supplied Ethernet cable.
4. Press and hold the power button for two seconds until the status LED turns green.

Troubleshooting: If the status LED blinks red, perform a factory reset by holding the
RESET button for ten seconds. Refer to the specifications table in Appendix A for
supported voltage ranges and operating temperatures.
